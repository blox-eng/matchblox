The desk

The matchblox console on a 16-inch desk, drawn cell by cell at 160×40 with the console's own glyphs and inks. matchblox draws the whole screen: the header on top, the bar at the bottom, the list, and the agent's real screen through a terminal built into matchblox. It draws today's half edge itself, and the sheets float over the agent. Demo names only. Click the screen, then ? for every key, , for the machine, t shell, D review.

The canon, in cells

A terminal has cells, not pixels. These rules give the desk its rhythm without anything the console does not already draw.

RuleOn the deskWhy
Gutter1 cell at the left of every pane. Column 0 is the selection's ▌; nothing else uses it.The real list already works this way. The eye finds the choice in one column.
Spacing scale0 cells between a glyph and its word's mark (✻│); 1 between a glyph and its word (╿ name); 2 inside a cluster (− after a count); 3 between clusters on the bar; 4 between spaces in the header (the tab row's rhythm).Five steps, each with one job. Gaps grow with the distance in meaning.
Separators· in faint, one space each side, inside a cluster. Never between clusters.As in the key line and the chain line today.
LinesA line in hair is a pane edge, or the rule under the header and over the bar. The only other line is the hairline between groups. Lines join where they meet (┬ ┴ ┤), and a joint takes the ink of the half of the edge it touches. No boxes, no badges with borders.One meaning per line. A box would read as a pane you can enter.
CornersOnly a sheet that floats has corners: a rounded border (╭╮╰╯) in faint, one step above a pane's hair, its title in the top edge, 1 cell of padding inside.A rounded edge says "floats, esc closes it". Square pane edges say "stays".
