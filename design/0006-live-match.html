The Jobs pass

What we took away, and why

No percentage on the lock screen.The match is the number. A figure next to it says the drawing failed. The expanded island can show it, because a long press asks for detail.
One match, not five.You can act on one thing at a time. The counts in the corner tell you the rest exists.
Only you make it move.Fire means work, smoke means you. Nothing else animates. That is DESIGN.md rule 1, on a lock screen.
No question text on the lock screen.Apple can't decrypt a Live Activity on the phone, so the relay would see it. The question goes in the encrypted alert. The activity only says who waits.
The tap opens the answer.The Answer button and a tap on the activity both open the answer peek of that session. There is no home screen in between.
Apple's rules we design around

"All the time" has fine print

An activity lives 8 hours.Then it stays on the lock screen for up to 4 more hours, frozen. Your host starts a new one with push-to-start (iOS 17.2 or later) before the old one ends, so it looks permanent.
Updates have a budget.Burn moves in steps of 5 % and at most every few minutes. A new wait goes out at once, with high priority. Done and out go out at once too.
The payload is visible to the relay.It carries a session number, the state and the progress. The phone turns the number into api from its own list, saved at the last connect. No names or text leave your host in the clear.
A session with no Progress line.The flame stays near the head and the wood stays whole. It still burns, so you know it works.
Yours to decide

Three calls before I write it into 0006

What does the wood measure? I propose task progress, as you asked. In the console a burnt match means a full context (│ spent). On the phone that is the stoker's job, and the work of the system is silent, so the phone does not show it.

One match or the box? I recommend the one match. The box can come later, behind a long press.
