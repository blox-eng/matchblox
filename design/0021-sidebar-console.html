Your feedback, and what this draft does

  1. Live view and ⏎. The chat is the main pane. Choosing a session puts your cursor in its line. ⌃o expands into the real pane; ⌃o again comes back, so esc still reaches the agent inside.
  2. Chains. Each row names its chain. The chosen session's chain is a strip over the chat. g shows only that chain, with the stoker's card.
  3. Phone. The list is the first screen. A tap opens the chat; ◂ comes back.
  4. Held vs quiet. HELD is its own group and says who held it and why.
  5. Setup, new session, tips. One row each at the foot of the sidebar. Each opens in the main pane.
  6. Hosts. s stays. Every host you added shows. A hidden host never makes s silent.
  7. The Jobs pass (cuts). No rail: the list is open or hidden, and hidden, the header names what needs you. One icon, the match. One line a session, two only for a wait and the chosen row. The agent's mark at the end of the line. No "no chain" line. One footer line. At most five keys on the key line.
  8. SHIPPING. The group goes. A pull request is a badge; one that waits for your merge puts its row in NEEDS YOU.

Open for you

  • The chat: a clean transcript, as drawn here, or the agent's real pane cut to its chat? I recommend the real pane: matchblox already reads panes, and a transcript needs a parser for each agent.
  • Chains: also a way to group the sidebar? I recommend not yet: the tag, the strip and g first.
  • Machine (usage, CPU, accounts): a row at the foot of the sidebar, or its own tab as today? I recommend the tab, reached with ⇥.
