## Lens findings

**Engineering, scope.** The plan has 24 tasks and about 12 new files, against a smell of 8 files and 2 new parts. The new parts are the agent's terminal (`agentterm`, with two new dependencies, the owner's wrapper ruling), the sheets and two acts (`seen:`, `diff:`). Cut: no new act for the send (Task 15 reuses `paste:`); no second key list (Task 11 reads `keys.go`); the Machine sheet reuses `machinePanel`. Kept, because the owner chose one PR with all of it.

**Engineering, architecture.** The service owns `StateAt`, `SeenAt`, the diff and the turn end. The console owns the mode, the space, the unsent comments and now the agent's screen (a pty with the nested tmux client of `ViewArgv`). The agent's pane stays in its own tmux session, so `⌃o`, recover and every other console see it as today. Failures in production, each with its task: a pane id changes after a recover, so seen and reviews key on the conversation id (Tasks 2 and 14); an old service sends no `StateAt` (Task 4); the nested client exits (Task 8); the agent prints a title, clipboard or query sequence (Task 8b); a paste of 20 KB (Task 8c); git runs a pager, a hook or an external diff (Task 13); a big diff (Task 13); the list's choice moves while a review is open (Task 15).

**Engineering, regression.** At risk: the phone's screen (Task 9 and Task 17 keep it byte-equal); HELD last (Task 3 keeps `TestTheListEndsInHeld`); `ctrl+q` and `⌃o`/`ctrl+a m` (Task 8c); `⇥` on the phone (Task 10); the chain rule (Task 3).

**Engineering, performance.** The agent's terminal parses every byte the agent prints and redraws at most 60 times a second; its CPU on a busy agent is unknown. The cheapest check: Task 8c measures key echo (p50 and p95), and the review (Task 20) samples the console's CPU while an agent streams a long diff. The diff runs only on `D`, with a 10 s timeout and a 2 MB cap.

**Design, every state.**
- Head row. Empty: one repo shows no spaces. Error: the service lost shows `no service · it redials`. 8; a 10 also names the space the owner is in when the list is hidden (no change, because the open space is in the accent).
